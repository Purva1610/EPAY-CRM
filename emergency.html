Quick guides

First steps you can take

General orientation only. Always follow local law and official instructions.

🛂 Lost passport

  1. File a police report where required.
  2. Contact your embassy/consulate for emergency travel document process.
  3. Keep copies/photos of passport and visas if available.
  4. Inform employer/university and your insurer.
  5. Request ePay case support for checklist & Gallery follow-up.
Tip: Digital copies in secure cloud help speed embassy processes.

✈️ Travel disruption

  1. Contact the airline/operator for rebooking rules.
  2. Check travel insurance coverage for delays.
  3. Secure safe temporary stay if stranded overnight.
  4. Keep receipts for potential claims.
  5. Open an ePay case if you need coordination help.
Tip: Screenshot booking refs and gate changes as evidence.

🏠 Accommodation emergency

  1. Prioritise personal safety — leave if unsafe.
  2. Document the issue (photos, messages, contract).
  3. Contact local emergency services if threatened.
  4. Seek temporary lodging via hotels / verified partners.
  5. Notify ePay for orientation and partner options where available.
Tip: Share only necessary details; keep originals of contracts.

🏥 Medical coordination

  1. For emergencies call local emergency number first.
  2. Contact your travel/health insurer helpline.
  3. Carry policy number and ID copies.
  4. ePay can help you locate insurer contacts and next admin steps.
  5. We do not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.
Tip: Save embassy and insurer numbers offline before travel.

What ePay does / doesn’t

Clear boundaries

✓ ePay can help with

  1. Structured case intake & priority triage
  2. Step-by-step checklists for common issues
  3. Pointers to embassy / insurer / partner contacts
  4. Coordination with verified service partners where available
  5. Gallery follow-up for ongoing document support

✗ ePay does not

  1. Replace police, hospitals or embassies
  2. Provide medical treatment or legal representation
  3. Guarantee visa, refund or employer outcomes
  4. Issue passports or official government documents
  5. Act as your insurer or bail authority
Partner model: Where temporary stay, local transport or documentation support is available, ePay may coordinate with verified providers. You remain responsible for verifying costs, contracts and eligibility.
