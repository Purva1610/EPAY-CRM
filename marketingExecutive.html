Can Do

  • Create marketing activities & execute assigned campaigns

  • Create events & submit campaign / daily reports

  • View assigned campaign performance & leads

  • Change CRM workflows or system configuration
  • Change user permissions
  • Access unauthorized campaigns
  • Override Finance / CFO controls
